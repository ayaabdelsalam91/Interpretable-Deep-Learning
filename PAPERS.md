# Interpretable Deep Learning - Reading List

A curated list of papers and resources focused on concept-based interpretability methods for deep learning.

**Contributing:** We welcome contributions! Please submit a pull request or open an issue to suggest papers.

---

## Concept-Based Explanations

Methods that explain models using high-level concepts, including Concept Bottleneck Models and related approaches.

### Foundational Papers

- **Interpretability Beyond Feature Attribution: Testing with Concept Activation Vectors (TCAV)**  
  Kim et al. (2018)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.11279)

- **Concept Bottleneck Models**  
  Koh et al. (2020)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.04612)

### Recent Advances

- **ConceptScope: Characterizing Dataset Bias via Disentangled Visual Concepts**  
  Choi et al. (2025)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26186)

- **Concept-Guided Interpretability via Neural Chunking**  
  Wu et al. (2025)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.11576)

- **Towards Compositional Interpretability for XAI**  
  Tull et al. (2024)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17583)

- **From Black-box to Causal-box: Towards Building More Interpretable Models**  
  Hwang, Pan & Bareinboim (2025)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21998)

- **Object Centric Concept Bottlenecks**  
  Steinmann et al. (2025)  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24492)

- **Interpretable Hierarchical Concept Reasoning through Attention-Guided Graph Learning**  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21102)

- **Safe Reinforcement Learning via Probabilistic Logic Shields**  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03226)

### Improving Concept Bottleneck Models

  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.17921)

- **Deferring Concept Bottleneck Models: Learning to Defer Interventions to Inaccurate Experts**  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16199)

- **Measuring Leakage In Concept-Based Methods: An Information Theoretic Approach**  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.09459)

  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16705)

- **Shortcuts and Identifiability in Concept-based Models from a Neuro-Symbolic Lens**  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11245)

### Novel Architectures and Extensions
