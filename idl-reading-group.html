About the Reading Group

This reading group brings together researchers and practitioners interested in interpretable deep learning. We discuss recent papers, share insights, and explore methods to make deep learning models more transparent and understandable. Each session features paper presentations, discussions, and Q&A, with recordings available on our YouTube channel.
